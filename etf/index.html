What is an ETF?

An ETF (Exchange Traded Fund) is a type of investment fund traded on stock exchanges, much like stocks. It tracks an index, commodity, bonds, or a basket of assets such as Nifty 50, Sensex, or Gold. When you buy ETF units, you are investing in a portfolio of securities that replicate the performance of its benchmark index. Unlike actively managed mutual funds, ETFs don’t aim to beat the market — they mirror it

Key Risks to Consider

  • Market volatility affects ETF prices
  • Tracking error may cause mismatch vs. index
  • Low-liquidity ETFs may face wider bid-ask spreads
  • No guaranteed returns, depends on index movement
