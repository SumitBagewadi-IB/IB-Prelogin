What is Margin Trading Facility?

Margin Trading Facility (MTF) by Indiabulls Securities Limited allows investors to buy Margin Trading Facility (MTF) by Indiabulls Securities Limited allows investors to buy stocks by paying only a portion of the total amount — the rest is funded by Indiabulls Securities Limited (ISL), allowing amplified gains but also bigger risks, as you pay interest on the borrowed amount until you close your position. It's a way to leverage your capital for bigger opportunities, with shares held in your Demat account but pledged to the broker i.e. ISL. MTF Facility is available for specific stocks as prescribed by the exchanges, requiring careful management of leverage and interest costs. MTF is regulated by bodies like SEBI, which provides a structured and monitored environment for this type of trading activity.

With our MTF, you can:

  • Trade in exchange-approved (NSE / BSE) approved equity stocks

  • Use cash or pledged holdings as collateral

  • Hold leveraged positions overnight or longer

  • Pay interest only on the borrowed amount

  • Monitor exposure in real-time across web & app

Frequently Asked Questions

MTF is a service provided by Indiabulls Securities limited that allows investors to buy stocks by paying only a portion of the total trade value. The remaining is funded by the us. This facility offers leverage, amplifying potential returns and risks.

Any investor with an active Trading and Demat account with Indiabulls Securities limited and who complies with SEBI guidelines is eligible for MTF.

The investor must maintain a minimum margin, which can be in the form of cash and/or approved securities. The margin percentage depends on the specific stock and broker’s internal policies.

Interest is calculated daily on the borrowed amount and is typically charged monthly or as per our billing cycle. The interest rate is set by the broker and is subject to change.

You can hold MTF positions as long as you maintain the required margin and meet all our and NSE conditions. We offer unlimited holding periods, subject to compliance.
