What is Stock SIP?

Stock SIP (Systematic Investment Plan in Stocks) is a way of investing in individual stocks regularly (like monthly, weekly, or daily) with a fixed amount, instead of buying a large quantity at once.

  • • Systematic Investment Plan
  • • Flexible Investment Frequency
  • • Simple and Automated process

Benefits of SIP

Disciplined Investing

Invest regularly without worrying about timing the market.

Rupee Cost Averaging

Buy stocks at different price levels, balancing highs & lows.

Wealth Creation

Build long-term wealth by accumulating quality stocks.

Flexibility

Start small & increase your investment anytime.

Convenience

Automated investments, no need to track daily.

Direct Ownership

You own the shares directly, unlike mutual funds.

Stock SIP vs Mutual Funds vs Lump Sum

Direct ownership of stocks Managed by professionals One-time investment Lower expense ratios Higher fees (1–2%) Higher exposure to market risk Full control over where you invest Limited customization Greater potential for quick gains or losses

Stock SIPs can outperform mutual funds by up to 1.5% annually—making it a smarter, goal-aligned, and disciplined way to invest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Stock SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) lets you invest a fixed amount or quantity in selected stocks at regular intervals.

Stock SIP promotes disciplined investing, helps average out purchase cost, and removes the need to time the market.

It is an instruction placed by an investor to automatically buy a stock in fixed amount or quantity on a set frequency.

Funds are generally required in the trading account before execution; they are not auto-debited from your bank.
