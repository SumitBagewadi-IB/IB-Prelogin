Frequently Asked Questions

A Smart Basket is a curated collection of stocks or ETFs bundled together based on a specific theme, strategy, or investment objective. Instead of picking individual stocks, you can invest in a professionally selected portfolio with just a few clicks, making investing simpler and more efficient.

Unlike mutual funds, where fund managers make decisions on your behalf, a Smart Basket gives you full transparency and control. You own the individual stocks in your demat account, can track performance in real-time, and have the flexibility to add, remove, or modify stocks at any time.

Each Smart Basket is created and regularly reviewed by registered SEBI advisors. These experts use a data-driven approach to curate portfolios aligned with market trends, sectors, or long-term strategies.

Simply log in to your Indiabulls Securities account, navigate to the Smart Basket section, choose a basket that aligns with your goals, and click 'Invest.' The stocks will be executed and added directly to your demat account in one go.
